Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When does MTI officially launch?

A: Fall 2026. Applications are being accepted now through Spring 2026.

Q: Can I visit the campus?

A: Yes! We're currently securing facilities. Contact us to schedule a visit once our location is finalized.

Q: How do I get started with biblical language study?

A: Check local seminaries, Bible colleges, or online programs. We recommend resources in our Scriptorium.

Q: Are you accepting international students?

A: Yes, but you must arrange your own visa (F-1 student visa). We're working toward SEVIS certification.

Q: What if I don't meet all prerequisites yet?

A: Contact us! We can help you create a plan to complete prerequisites before Fall 2026.

Q: Do you offer financial aid?

A: Yes—scholarships, church sponsorships, sending organization funding. Veterans use GI Bill (full coverage).
