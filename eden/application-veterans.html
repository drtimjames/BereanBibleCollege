You must have completed at least two semesters (one academic year) of Biblical Hebrew, Koine Greek, or Biblical Aramaic with a minimum grade of B (80%) or better.

Section 7: Cross-Cultural & Missionary Experience

Modified Prerequisite for Veterans

Standard Berean Requirement: 3-6 months of missionary field experience under a career missionary.

Veterans Adjustment: Your combat deployment provides partial credit for cross-cultural experience. However, you must ALSO complete missionary field experience under a career missionary to demonstrate calling to Gospel ministry (not just ability to endure hardship).

Accepted Combinations:

  • Combat deployment (completed) + 3-6 months missionary experience (completed)
  • Combat deployment (completed) + Short-term mission trip (2-4 weeks) + Commitment to complete 3-6 month missionary stint before starting at Berean

Section 9: Veterans Essay (Modified Great Commission Essay)

REQUIRED: Submit a 1,500-word essay addressing the prompts below.

Essay Prompts for Veterans (address all four):

1. Military Service & Cross-Cultural Experience: What did you learn about cross-cultural ministry through your military deployment(s)? How has this prepared you for missionary work?

2. From Warrior to Missionary: How has your identity shifted from military service member to missionary? What skills transfer? What must change?

3. Evidence of Calling: What specific evidence do you have that God has called you from military service to missionary service? (Provide concrete examples, not just feelings.)

4. Why Berean?: Why Berean Bible College specifically? How does our intensive, "Languages First" approach appeal to someone with your military background?

Essay must be 1,500 words (+/- 100 words). Format: 12pt font, double-spaced, 1-inch margins.
