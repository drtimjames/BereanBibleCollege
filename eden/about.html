About the Missionary Training Institute

As an expression of our obedience to God and our love for our fellow man, missionaries share the Gospel even though that may come at great personal cost — "even unto death" (Matthew 28:18-20, Revelation 12:11).

We are not a traditional Bible college. We are an elite missionary training institute that demands excellence, requires proven commitment, and produces graduates who read Scripture in its original language and context.

"Languages first. Context always. Truth above tradition. Excellence for His glory."
— The Berean Standard

Our Founder: Dr. Tim James

Why Berean Bible College Is Different

1. Languages First

"If you don't understand the original language… how can you comprehend the context of the original author... or of God Himself?!"

Most Bible colleges teach students about the Bible before teaching them to read the Bible. We require Biblical language proficiency before admission.
