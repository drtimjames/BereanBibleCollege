Statement of Faith

Humanity is earthbound (John 3:13, 8:23, 13:33). Jesus came once for the forgiveness of sins for all who would believe (1 Corinthians 15:3). That was important, but the best is yet to take place. Jesus is coming again - and when He arrives - He'll be here to stay! Humanity is binary: i.e., either righteous or unrighteous. The righteous will inherit the earth as co-heirs of Jesus (Matthew 5:5, Psalm 37:29, Revelation 20:4-6, Revelation 22:1-5). Upon mortal death, the unrighteous have no further opportunity for redemption (Hebrews 9:27). The judgment (Gr. bēma) seat (2 Corinthians 2:14-17,5:10) is where the righteous are judged for how productively they employed their gifts and talents to build Jesus' kingdom - and that will determine one's "crown of righteousness" - 1 Corinthians 9:25: our imperishable wreath is our millennial role in making "all things new again", as it was in the beginning in Eden's garden. Ultimately, the earth will be restored to its original Edenic condition. The Father will deliver the new Jerusalem to the earth, and He will finally dwell with righteous humanity, as He had initially planned from the beginning.

About Berean Bible College and Missionary Training Institute

As an expression of our obedience to God and our love for our fellow man, missionaries share the Gospel even though that may come at great personal cost - "even unto death" (Matthew 28:18-20, Revelation 12:11). Your Great Commission calling may direct you to your own home country, or it may involve crossing cultural barriers and geographic boundaries.

We are not a traditional Bible college. We are an elite missionary training institute that demands excellence, requires proven commitment, and produces graduates who read Scripture in its original language and context.

"Languages first. Context always. Truth above tradition. Excellence for His glory."
— The Berean Standard

Our Founder: Dr. Tim James

Dr. Tim James is an author, Bible scholar, and missionary with extensive international and cross-cultural experience. In addition, Tim is an adventurer, and he and his family have lived aboard and crossed oceans on their sailboats.

Missionary Service & Cross-Cultural Ministry

Tim and his wife Jeanne have served in missionary capacities overseas. Their service has included:

  • Training at the IMB's Missionary Training Center in Virginia (twice)
  • In 1996, Dr. James became the first in his surgical specialty to be licensed in Kenya, and in 2000, he became licensed in the UK
  • Extended ministry in East Africa and North Africa-Middle East regions, and Europe
  • Founded and operates Children's Health, Education, and Leadership Project (H.E.L.P.), an American 501(c)(3) non-profit organization since 1999, as well as a Spanish NGO (Spain-N. Africa)
  • Numerous medical and disaster relief missions to China, Kenya, Papua New Guinea, Turkey, and Morocco.
  • Cross-cultural church planting, youth and community teaching ministries
  • Partnership with international missionary sending organizations

Tim is a life-long Christian raised by a Roman Catholic mother and Presbyterian father. Jeanne was raised in the Catholic and Lutheran traditions. During their various stints overseas, they developed close relationships with Bible-believing Christians from all around the globe. They raised their young family to be Holy Spirit-guided born-again Christians without loyalty to any particular denomination. Tim's motto: "In math, denominators are dividers. In Christianity, denominations function likewise." Their cross-cultural experience profoundly shaped their understanding of Scripture and God's redemptive plan. Further, they came to appreciate what the author of Acts 17:11-12 meant about the Berean Jews: "they received the word with all eagerness, examining the Scriptures daily to see if these things were so. Many of them, therefore, believed." And that's what God expects of us: To conduct our own due diligence. Learn Hebrew, Greek, and a little Aramaic. Don't rely solely on English mistranslations lest you pick up false theologies.

Academic Training

Dr. James is a graduate of Heidelberg University (Ohio), and he also studied at Kent State and Cleveland State Universities. Tim received his medical degree from Ohio College of Podiatric Medicine in Cleveland in 1977. His specialty: Foot and Ankle Surgery. His hospital residency was performed in Long Beach, California. Vision for Berean Bible College

Drawing on decades of missionary experience and biblical study, Dr. James founded Berean Bible College to train missionaries who:

  • Read Scripture in its original languages (Hebrew, Greek, Aramaic)
  • Understand the Bible in its ancient Near Eastern and Second Temple contexts
  • Embrace biblical restoration theology and new creation hope
  • Are prepared for cross-cultural missionary service in any environment
  • Are willing to endure faithfully—even unto death—for the Gospel

Why Berean Bible College Is Different

1. Languages First

"If you don't understand the original language, and you don't understand what was happening in the world at the time those words were written, how can you comprehend the context of the original author... or of God, for that matter?!"

Most Bible colleges teach students about the Bible before teaching them to read the Bible. We require Biblical language proficiency before admission, so from day one you're engaging Scripture in Hebrew and Greek—not English translations.

2. Context Always

Scripture was written in specific historical, cultural, and linguistic contexts. We immerse students in the Ancient Near East, Second Temple Judaism, and the Greco-Roman world so they can read Scripture as its original audiences did.

3. Restoration Theology

God's plan is not evacuation to heaven. His plan is restoration—the renewal of creation, the Millennium, the New Jerusalem, and eternity on the renewed Earth. This biblical hope radically changes how we understand mission and salvation.

4. Missionary Focus

We train missionaries—not pastors or Bible teachers. Students must prove their calling through prerequisite field experience (like the IMB's Missionary Training Center). We partner with sending organizations to deploy graduates immediately.

5. Excellence for God's Glory

We demand rigorous academic standards and deep spiritual formation. Our graduates represent Christ to the nations—they must be prepared at the highest level.

Not Your Average Bible College

Berean Bible College is selective by design.

There are hundreds of Bible colleges that accept anyone and teach basic theology in English. Those schools serve a purpose. But we exist for students who:

  • Have already proven their language proficiency and missionary calling
  • Want to read Scripture in Hebrew and Greek, not secondhand translations
  • Are willing to complete three years of intensive, year-round study
  • Have completed prerequisite missionary training (like IMB's MTC)
  • Are prepared to deploy to the mission field and endure faithfully

If you meet those standards, welcome.

If you don't yet, go get the prerequisites. Then come back.

Statement of Faith

We believe:

  • The Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God in its original languages.
  • One God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, died as substitutionary atonement for sin, rose bodily, and ascended to the Father.
  • Salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone.
  • The Church is called to proclaim the Gospel to all nations until Christ returns.
  • The Great Commission is the mission of the Church.
  • The Second Coming of Christ to judge, establish His kingdom, and renew creation.
  • The Resurrection of believers unto eternal life on the renewed Earth.
  • The Restoration of All Things—God's plan is restoration, not evacuation. Eternity will be on the renewed Earth.
