📚 ABHE ACCREDITATION PREREQUISITES:
MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS TO APPLY:
1. INSTITUTIONAL STABILITY

Institution must be operating for at least 2 years before applying
Must have graduated at least one class of students
Must demonstrate financial viability (audited financial statements)
Must have a permanent physical location (or clear plan if online)

2. GOVERNANCE & ADMINISTRATION

Board of Trustees (at least 5 members, majority must be independent)
Clear organizational structure (President, Academic Dean, etc.)
Bylaws and governance documents
Statement of Faith clearly defined
Non-profit status (501(c)(3) required)

3. ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

Defined curriculum (course catalog, syllabi, credit hours)
Qualified faculty (appropriate terminal degrees for their fields)
Learning outcomes defined and assessed
Library resources (physical or digital access to theological resources)
Student services (admissions, registration, advising, career services)

4. FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS

501(c)(3) tax-exempt status (must apply through IRS)
Annual independent audit by CPA
Budget showing revenue/expenses
Sustainable financial model (tuition + donations cover operations)
Application fee: $3,500 (non-refundable)
Annual dues: $2,500-$10,000 depending on size

5. FACILITIES & RESOURCES

Physical campus OR clear online delivery model
Library (minimum 10,000 volumes or equivalent digital access)
Technology infrastructure (LMS if online)
Administrative offices


🗓️ ABHE ACCREDITATION TIMELINE:
PHASE 1: Pre-Candidacy (2+ years)

Operate as an institution
Build student enrollment
Establish governance structure
Develop curriculum
Hire faculty
Graduate first class

PHASE 2: Candidacy Application (Year 3)

Submit application to ABHE
Pay application fee ($3,500)
Host site visit from ABHE evaluators
Receive Candidacy status (if approved)
Candidacy period: 2-7 years

PHASE 3: Full Accreditation (Year 5-10)

Demonstrate compliance with all standards
Host comprehensive evaluation visit
Receive full accreditation (if approved)
Reaccreditation: Every 10 years

TOTAL TIMELINE: 5-10 years from founding to full accreditation

💰 ABHE COSTS (Estimated):

Site Visit Fee: $3,000-$5,000 (covers evaluator travel/expenses)
Annual Membership Dues: $2,500-$10,000 (based on enrollment)
Reaccreditation Visit: $5,000 (every 10 years)

Total first 5 years: ~$25,000-$40,000
