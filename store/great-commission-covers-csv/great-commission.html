PILGRIMAGES The Camino de Santiago de Compostela, also known as "The Way," is an ancient pilgrimage that has been undertaken dating back to the era of the Crusades. The Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela is located in northwestern Spain, and it is reputed to be the burial place of Saint James, the apostle. There are several routes ranging from shorter 115 km to over 800 km in length, and requiring from a week to over a month to complete. Jeanne and I have hiked the Camino twice, taking different routes each time. Being in our late 60s and early 70s, we opted to have a delivery service pick up our one heavier backpack and deliver it to our next destination. We each carried only a daypack weighing around 5 pounds. We opted to stay in 3- to 5-star hotels most nights, so having an agent handle your reservations and arrange reliable taxi service was most convenient and well worth the nominal cost. Our typical daily routine was to get up early, eat breakfast, and be on the trail around sunrise.
