Minsk-area rail terminal opens with China-bound export freight

GREATPORT opened on 6 November. Its rail partner reported a test arrival from Xi’an and a China-bound export train routed via Russia and Kazakhstan.

What began operating

GREATPORT, a multimodal rail terminal at the Great Stone Industrial Park outside Minsk, opened on 6 November 2026. UTLC ERA reported on 9 November that a test train carrying automotive components from Xi’an arrived on 28 October and that the terminal dispatched a China-bound container train on opening day. Xinhua reported the opening and outbound departures on 7 November.

UTLC ERA said the opening-day train carried 62 forty-foot containers of Belarusian exports, including milk, flax and wood products. The operator identified buyers in Jiangsu, Shaanxi and Henan and said the train would travel through Russia and Kazakhstan. Arrival at the Chinese destinations has yet to be verified.

Why it matters

The terminal adds a handling point for an existing Eurasian rail route serving Chinese trade and using Russian rail infrastructure. The inbound test and outbound departure establish initial use. The cooperation memorandum signed at the opening is a separate milestone. These sources give no sustained throughput, regular departure frequency or evidence of cargo shifting from ocean shipping.

What to watch next

When does the first outbound consignment arrive? Will operators publish a regular timetable and actual container volumes? How do border transfer times and costs compare with other routes?
