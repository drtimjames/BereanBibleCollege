Tanker strikes river cruise boat, closing part of the Mississippi

The Atlantic Asphalt struck the docked American Heritage in New Orleans. The Coast Guard closed the river between mile markers 93 and 96.

What happened

On 13 November, the asphalt tanker Atlantic Asphalt struck the American Heritage, an American Cruise Lines paddlewheel boat carrying 129 passengers. The cruise boat rolled onto its side and was partially submerged. Emergency crews and nearby vessels assisted in the rescue.

What remains uncertain

The cruise line said it was working with the Coast Guard. The cause, the tanker’s cargo status and the timing of a reopening remained unconfirmed in the reports reviewed.

Why it matters and what to watch

The closure interrupts navigation through a section of the Lower Mississippi. Watch for the Coast Guard’s reopening notice and any restrictions during salvage.
