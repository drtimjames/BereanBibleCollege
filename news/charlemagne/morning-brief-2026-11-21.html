The Day Watch Morning Brief

Tanker strike reports near Oman, a tentative Pacific ports agreement, and the evidence still needed on shipping access, farm services and protection for Syrian Christians.

Where has risk eased, and where do people still need evidence that access or services have recovered?

UKMTO reports two tanker strike incidents near Oman

Two UK Maritime Trade Operations notices dated November 21 describe projectile strikes on outbound tankers near Khasab. Neither notice reported casualties at the time. Environmental impact, the vessels’ identities and whether the reports involved the same tanker remain unconfirmed. Hormuz serves major oil buyers including China and India. Watch for operator statements, damage findings and changes to official transit guidance.

Tentative Los Angeles–Long Beach deal eases strike risk

The Pacific Maritime Association said November 21 it had reached a tentative agreement with ILWU Local 26, Reuters reported. The employer group said the existing contract would be extended pending a vote. The watchmen provide security at container terminals, including gate access. Shippers now have a reason to expect continuity. A union ratification result and terminal operating notices will show what follows.

Port order restricts part of the Kerch shipping channel

A November 18 order from the Russian port administration in occupied Crimea restricts vessel navigation on a specified section of the Kerch–Yenikale Canal from November 19 until further notice. The document cites a navigational hazard. This passage connects the Sea of Azov with the Black Sea and serves Russian grain exporters. Effects on individual cargo voyages remain unknown. Watch for a superseding order and verified freight transits.

USDA works to restore farm loan and payment tools

On November 20, USDA Undersecretary Richard Fordyce told reporters that a cyber threat disrupted agency systems and that no data was lost, DTN reported. Farm loan, payment and conservation tools were among the reported outages. Fordyce expected most systems back by Friday evening, with weekend work if needed. Full restoration and any payment backlog remain unconfirmed. Watch for a service-by-service agency update.

DTN’s on-the-record reporting · Full report

Syrian church leaders seek protection after Homs attack

Melkite Greek Catholic Archbishop Jean-Abdo Arbach confirmed to Aid to the Church in Need on November 17 that he was safe following the November 14 attack and robbery. In a joint appeal dated November 17 and covered by Vatican News on November 20, three Syrian patriarchs called for investigations, protection and equal citizenship. The perpetrators and motive remain unresolved. Watch for investigative findings and concrete protection measures.

Baltic shipping update

21 November 2026, 10:33 a.m. Pacific

What changed. Finland lifted wider temporary air and sea restrictions in the eastern Gulf of Finland. Its Border Guard set a separate safety zone around the damaged Russian cargo ship Sparta II. The confirmed limits of the restrictions narrow the immediate concern to the damaged voyage and rescue area. Watch for a salvage update and any cancellations on the St. Petersburg–Kaliningrad route.
