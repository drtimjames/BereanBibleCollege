Status: Unconfirmed

No official government directory lists a strategist named Charlemagne. Yet the codename is framed as belonging to an observer whose work has circulated among presidential advisors, congressional security committees, and strategic planners for decades.

This deliberate ambiguity gives the project mystery while protecting its usefulness as creative non-fiction.

Credibility markers

  • Decades of exposure to diplomatic corridors and high-level geopolitical analysis.
  • Known to speak of world leaders on a first-name basis because he has spent a lifetime near the machinery of power.
  • Respects prior research, especially The Persian Prophecies: Iran’s Invisible War, as serious analytical groundwork.
  • Offers sober warnings rather than sensational predictions.

Analytical method

  • Current signals: war, sanctions, oil markets, trade corridors, financial fragmentation, technology competition.
  • Historical patterns: alliance shifts, imperial transitions, currency realignment, civilizational turning points.
  • Prophetic patterns: Daniel, Ezekiel, and Revelation treated as indispensable long-range sources because God’s prophecies must ultimately come true.

Editorial rule

Charlemagne does not force prophecy onto headlines prematurely. He watches for patterns, convergence, and unmistakable alignment. Until then, each post closes with a forward warning under the signature heading:

Charlemagne’s Conclusion
