Russian strikes kill two and injure 48 in Zaporizhzhia, regional authorities say

A five-story apartment building was partly destroyed. The regional administration’s updated count follows an earlier city report of 32 injured. Municipal crews are assessing damage and making emergency repairs.

What officials reported

Zaporizhzhia regional authorities said two people were killed and 48 injured after overnight Russian strikes on 24 November. The Kyiv Independent reported the updated regional count. The Associated Press reported an earlier count of 44 injured, including four children. Acting regional administration head Mykhailo Semikin said a private home was destroyed and a five-story apartment building partly destroyed.

Zaporizhzhia’s city council said buildings in two districts were damaged. Its assessment as of 7 a.m. local time listed 18 apartment buildings and five private homes. The council said crews were covering broken windows and inspecting nearby properties for further damage.

Why it matters

The number of buildings affected gives some indication of the cleanup and repair work, including at homes that sustained less damage than the partly collapsed block. The early damage inventory does not establish the total number of displaced households or the duration of any interruptions to city services.

What to watch

Will regional officials revise the casualty count again? How many families need temporary housing, and when will municipal crews complete safety inspections and restore access?
