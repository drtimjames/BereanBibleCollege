Norway seeks to expand Ukraine’s drone defenses after Oslo talks

Norway says donated air-defense systems are already in use in Ukraine and wants to widen coverage. The ministers also signed a separate letter of intent allowing Norway to test Ukrainian command software.

What changed

Norwegian Defense Minister Tore O. Sandvik and Ukrainian Defense Minister Evgeniy Khmara met in Oslo on 24 November. Norway’s Defense Ministry said it had acquired and donated several jointly developed DESNA air-defense systems in recent months and that Ukraine already uses them. Sandvik said his government is working to expand the project so it can protect more areas. The ministry gave no new delivery count or timetable.

The ministers also signed a letter of intent that allows Norway’s armed forces to test Ukraine’s DELTA software. Norway described this as a step toward testing. Ukraine’s Defense Ministry described broader cooperation on implementation. The announcements give no schedule for Norwegian operational adoption.

Funding and deliveries

Ukraine’s ministry said it expects at least US$7 billion in Norwegian bilateral assistance in 2027, with air defense and unmanned systems among the priorities. Norway’s earlier government budget proposal sought NOK 85 billion for military and civilian aid under its Nansen programme. The Oslo announcements give no project-specific allocation or delivery schedule for the proposed DESNA expansion.

Why it matters

The proposed expansion would build on systems already in use, according to Oslo. The practical question for communities exposed to drone attacks is how quickly additional deliveries can extend protection. Norway’s planned software test also gives Ukraine a role as a technology partner in the relationship.

What to watch

Will Norway publish a funded expansion, an order or a delivery schedule? What will the Norwegian armed forces report after testing DELTA? Confidence is high in the announced talks and signed letter of intent. The timeline, scale and effect of the expansion remain open.
