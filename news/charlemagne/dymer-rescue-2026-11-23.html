Dymer death toll rises to 12 after rescued woman dies, Kyiv region says

Kyiv’s regional administration said a 79-year-old woman rescued from the rubble died in hospital. Search and rescue ended on 23 November, and local authorities are assessing damage and helping displaced residents.

What changed

Updated 24 November 2026, 4:48 a.m. Pacific

The Kyiv Regional Military Administration announced on 24 November that the death toll from the 22 November strike had risen to 12. Regional governor Tymur Tkachenko said the 79-year-old woman rescued alive from the rubble later died in hospital. The administration had announced the end of search and rescue on 23 November, when it reported 11 dead.

The region says it is documenting losses and helping households apply for compensation. It also reported plans to restore utilities in a damaged part of the building within two to three days. The full extent of displacement and the timetable for permanent housing remain open.

Earlier account · 23 November

On 23 November local time, regional governor Tymur Tkachenko reported that the death toll had risen to 11. The Kyiv Regional Military Administration said a Russian drone struck a five-story residential building and that search-and-rescue work was still underway. The latest notice gave no remaining count of people believed trapped.

Rescue and relief

Reuters reported that rescuers brought out a seven-year-old boy and a 79-year-old woman alive, citing emergency service spokeswoman Viktoriia Ruban. She said air-raid alerts and the continuing threat of drones interrupted the search. Local authorities opened a support center with social services, volunteers and mental-health support for residents.

Why it matters

Repeated alerts can force rescuers to pause work even when survivors may still be under rubble. Dymer’s response shows the need to sustain rescue and housing support at the same time. The public record establishes the damage to the building and the emergency response. The latest official toll is 12. The extent of displacement remains open.

What to watch

When will utilities return, and how many households receive temporary housing, compensation or repair assistance?
