Panama Canal: more booking access, greater permitted draft

Event and reporting date: 9 November 2026. Source: Panama Canal Authority, Advisory A-42-2026, Vice President for Operations Boris Moreno Vásquez.

Confirmed: ACP adds one Panamax Super-category daily booking slot, increasing that category from 18 to 19 and total daily booking capacity to 34. The added slot is offered for transit dates beginning 12 November through staged reservation arrangements. Maximum Neopanamax draft rises immediately to 15.24 metres (50 feet), until further notice.

Authority assessment: ACP attributes the changes to improving water conditions, favorable hydrological projections and water-saving measures. A reservation remains the only guarantee of a transit date; vessels without one may face extensive delays.

Verified BRICS relevance: ACP’s FY2025 annual report ranks China second among canal user countries by cargo tonnage. The connection is Chinese trade exposure, not a BRICS policy or preferential access arrangement.

Day Watch analysis: Another reservation slot improves scheduling access; greater permitted draft can enable eligible ships to carry heavier loads. Lower unit costs are possible, but no freight-rate reduction is established. This is an operating-rule change, not completed infrastructure expansion or a permanent water-supply guarantee.

Charlemagne follow-up: Do auction prices and waiting times fall? Do operators increase cargo loads on China-linked routes? Can the higher draft persist through the next dry season?
