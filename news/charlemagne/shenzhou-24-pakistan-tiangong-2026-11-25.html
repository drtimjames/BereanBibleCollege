Pakistani astronaut becomes first foreign crew member aboard China’s Tiangong station

China’s space agency confirmed that Shenzhou-24 docked on 25 November Beijing time. Payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali joined two Chinese astronauts on the mission.

What happened

The China Manned Space Agency said the spacecraft lifted off from Jiuquan at 8:18 a.m. Beijing time and docked at 11:56 a.m. on 25 November. Both events occurred on the evening of 24 November Pacific. The agency named Tang Hongbo as commander, Wu Yi as flight engineer and Muhammad Zeeshan Ali as payload specialist. Pakistan’s government said Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal attended the launch. Reuters reported Ali became the first foreign national aboard Tiangong.

Why it matters

A Pakistani crew member’s arrival puts China–Pakistan crewed spaceflight cooperation into practice. Pakistan’s government framed the flight as a milestone in the bilateral relationship. The completed launch and docking establish participation in a single mission. The scale of future astronaut exchanges and access to the station remains open.

What to watch

Confidence is high in the launch, docking and crew identities. Watch for official updates on Ali’s research, his return schedule and any commitment to a subsequent joint flight.
