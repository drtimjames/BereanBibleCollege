The Day Watch Morning Brief

Protecting the world's energy lifelines

Persian Gulf — Protecting commercial shipping

The Joint Maritime Information Center reported Thursday that 17 tankers had been struck in the Strait of Hormuz since November 1. Commercial shipping remains severely restricted, although vessels continue to transit.

American-assisted passages are continuing, providing an important measure of protection for maritime commerce.

Maritime security reporting · November 27

Saudi Arabia — Energy infrastructure under pressure

A projectile struck Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery, temporarily disrupting some operations. Saudi air defenses have also intercepted missiles and drones during recent Houthi attacks.

The distinction between intercepted attacks and confirmed infrastructure damage remains important.

The National · November 26

Global energy — Markets respond

Reuters reported Friday morning that Brent crude was trading at approximately $95.20 per barrel, with West Texas Intermediate at $83.74.

OPEC+ is scheduled to meet Sunday. Meanwhile, rising American crude inventories provide some protection against international supply pressures.

Reuters · November 27
