Nigerian Army says 18 children rescued in Kaduna, four still sought

The army said the children were among 22 Boys’ and Girls’ Brigade members abducted during a group walk on 22 November. It said the search for the remaining four continued.

What changed

Acting Army public relations director Col. Appolonia Anele said troops responded to a distress call in the Mother-Cat community in Kaduna’s Kachia/Kajuru area. The army reported rescuing 18 children and said efforts continued to find four others and arrest the abductors. Channels Television and Peoples Gazette reported the army’s statement. The latest account reviewed provides no further confirmed recovery or arrest.

Why it matters

The children belonged to the Boys’ and Girls’ Brigade, a Christian youth organization. The army described the abductors as suspected terrorists. Its statement gave no confirmed identity or motive. The immediate priorities are locating those still missing and supporting the children already rescued.

What to watch

Will the army, police or community representatives confirm that the remaining children are safe? What care and family support will be available to those rescued?
