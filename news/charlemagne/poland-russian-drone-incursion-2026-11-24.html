Poland says Russian drone briefly crossed its airspace; public alert lifted

Poland’s military said the drone crossed during Russian attacks on western Ukraine. A deputy defence minister called the incursion deliberate. Poland lifted the local air-attack alert late on 23 November.

What Polish authorities reported

Poland’s Armed Forces Operational Command said a Russian unmanned aircraft briefly entered Polish airspace on 23 November during a Russian attack on western Ukraine. The command reported tracking the aircraft and said it changed course. Poland’s Government Centre for Security issued an air-attack warning to part of Lublin province and lifted it at 11:54 p.m. local time.

Attribution and uncertainty

On 24 November, Deputy Defence Minister Paweł Zalewski described the crossing as deliberate, according to Polish Radio and Reuters. Another deputy minister described the aircraft as a surveillance drone, Reuters reported. Those are Polish officials’ assessments. The public military statement gives no flight duration or aircraft model. Reuters said the Russian embassy did not immediately respond to its request for comment.

Why it matters

The incident triggered a public shelter warning in part of a NATO member state while fighting was underway across the border. Authorities have ended that warning. The separate questions now are what evidence supports the assessment of intent and whether Poland or NATO announces a change in policy after reviewing the incident.

What to watch

Will Polish authorities release more evidence about the flight or publish an after-action account? Does NATO or the Polish government announce a change in air-defence or civilian warning arrangements?
