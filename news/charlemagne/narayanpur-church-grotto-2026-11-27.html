Police investigate suspected arson at Catholic parish site in Narayanpur

Police registered a case after an apparent attempt to set fire to a statue of Mary at Sacred Heart Church in Chhattisgarh, India. Investigators are working to identify the suspects.

What changed

On 27 November, Narayanpur Superintendent of Police Sandeep Patel told The Indian Express that police had registered a First Information Report against unidentified individuals and were investigating. The newspaper reported that the statue at the Viswadeepti High School compound was intact with visible soot marks. An official speaking to Press Trust of India placed the incident on the night of 24–25 November.

What the parish reported

Father Jomon Devasia of Sacred Heart Church told UCA News that security footage showed people setting fire to the statue and that someone had been seen kicking it earlier. The parish account dates the fire to early on 24 November. The exact time and extent of damage remain unclear. Police had announced no arrests in the reporting reviewed on 27 November. The identity and motive of the people involved remain undetermined.

Why it matters and what to watch

The same church compound was vandalized in January 2023. Another incident at the site has raised concern among local Christians ahead of Christmas. Look for police findings on the footage, identification of suspects, and any measures the parish or authorities announce to protect worshippers.
