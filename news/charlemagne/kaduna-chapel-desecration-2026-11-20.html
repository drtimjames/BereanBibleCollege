Kaduna archbishop calls for prayer and fasting after chapel desecration

Nigeria’s Catholic Secretariat documented the archdiocese’s response on 20 November and said a date would be announced for a Mass at the chapel.

What happened

Archbishop Matthew Man-oso Ndagoso said Christ the King Chapel at the Federal Training Centre in Malali, Kaduna, was vandalized on 14 November. He reported that consecrated Communion hosts and vessels, including a chalice and ciborium, were stolen. In his pastoral letter on 17 November, he asked Catholics across the archdiocese to fast and pray and announced plans for a Mass of reparation at the chapel.

Why it matters

The chapel serves students and staff of the training centre as well as other local worshippers. The theft involved objects central to Catholic worship, and the archdiocese is organizing a wider response. Confidence is high in the church’s account of its own response. The sources reviewed identify neither the perpetrators nor their motive, and they provide no public police findings.

What we’re watching

When will the Mass take place? Will the archdiocese report any change to regular services or access to the chapel? Will Kaduna police publish a case update or evidence about who was responsible and why?
